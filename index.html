OUR LITTLE CORNER OF THE UNIVERSE

Good energy only.

Be kind. No bullying, threats, hate, sexual content, or sharing someone else's private information.

Use a nickname. Do not share your address, school, phone number, live location, or passwords. If something feels wrong, report it and tell a trusted adult.

Only share content you own or have permission to use. External images and videos are loaded only when you choose; their host can see your network address.

A basic English word filter helps, but it will miss things. Report harmful posts or comments for human review. The administrator can remove posts and ban accounts.

Community beta: no direct messages, location sharing, ads, or autoplay. There are no guarantees of availability or perfect moderation. Uploaded videos are public; only share videos you have permission to publish. The Zaps feed uses categories, tags, playback and reactions, with controls to reset or disable personalization.
